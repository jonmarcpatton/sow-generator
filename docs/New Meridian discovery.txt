Discovery Call Notes: Meridian Logistics Group
Date: July 10, 2026
Attendees: Dana Ruiz (VP Operations), Marcus Feld (Director, IT), Priya Nadar (Support Lead)
Project Name: Support Escalation & Account Visibility

1. Overview & Business Objectives

Meridian Logistics operates a freight brokerage platform serving mid-market shippers.
Their support organization is drowning in escalations that arrive with no account context.
Agents cannot tell whether an inbound ticket is from a customer on a $2M annual contract
or a self-serve trial, so everything gets triaged the same way and their largest accounts
are experiencing the same response times as their smallest.

The primary objective is to give the support team account context at the point of triage,
and to eliminate the manual handoff between support and engineering when a ticket turns
out to be a platform defect rather than a user issue.

2. Key Integration Requirements & Use Cases

- Salesforce Integration (CRM to Support):
  - The Problem: Contract value, renewal date, and account tier live in Salesforce and
    are invisible to the support team at triage time.
  - The Requirement: Real-time sync of account attributes into the support queue.
  - Routing Logic: Tickets from accounts flagged "Strategic" or with a renewal inside
    90 days should route to the senior support pod automatically.

- Jira Integration (Support to Engineering):
  - The Problem: When support identifies a genuine platform defect, an agent re-types
    the whole ticket into Jira by hand. Priya estimates this costs the team 6-8 hours
    a week and introduces transcription errors.
  - The Requirement: One-click escalation from a support ticket to a Jira issue,
    preserving the original ticket content and customer identifiers.
  - Routing Logic: Jira issue priority should inherit from the account tier established
    by the Salesforce sync.

- Slack Integration (Notifications):
  - The Problem: Nobody finds out an escalation happened until someone checks a
    dashboard. Marcus specifically asked for this.
  - The Requirement: Post escalation notifications to a designated Slack channel,
    with the account name, tier, and Jira link.

- Professional Services:
  - Consulting: Meridian's team has never done a Salesforce integration and Marcus
    was explicit that they want architectural review before they start building
    against the API.
  - Training: Support leads need to understand how the routing rules work well enough
    to modify them without filing a ticket with us every time.

- Salesforce: Enterprise Edition. REST API access confirmed available.
- Jira: Jira Cloud. Marcus confirmed webhooks are permitted under their security policy.
- Slack: Enterprise Grid. App installation requires IT security review, which Marcus

- Timeline: Kickoff targeted for early August. Dana wants the Salesforce piece live
